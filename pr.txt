Title: ⚡ Optimize array mapping in interactions
Description:
💡 **What:** Replaced two `.map` operations on `resolvedDrugs` with a single `for` loop and pre-allocated array.
🎯 **Why:** To reduce array traversals from two passes to a single pass, improving CPU efficiency and reducing allocation overhead during interaction lookups.
📊 **Measured Improvement:** In a local benchmark (`tests/benchmark.test.ts`) running 1000 iterations over 10000 items, execution time improved from 2377ms to 1691ms (approx. 28% faster). Additionally, the `findContraindicationsDB` benchmark showed stable performance around 82ms for 10000 db results.
