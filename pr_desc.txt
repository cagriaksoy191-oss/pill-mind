🎯 **What:** Hardcoded static salt in AES-256-CBC session encryption.
⚠️ **Risk:** A hardcoded salt significantly reduces the security of the encryption, making the key derivation predictable. If the JWT_SECRET is compromised or guessed, attackers could more easily pre-compute or derive the keys to forge session tokens or decrypt intercepted sessions.
🛡️ **Solution:** Updated `encryptSession` in `lib/auth.ts` to dynamically generate a 16-byte random salt using `crypto.randomBytes(16).toString("hex")` during encryption. This salt is prepended to the generated token (`salt:iv:encrypted`). Updated `decryptSession` to extract and use this dynamically generated salt for key derivation. Added robust backward compatibility to ensure that existing legacy tokens (both those without an IV and intermediate ones with an IV but a hardcoded salt) continue to be successfully decrypted without logging users out.
